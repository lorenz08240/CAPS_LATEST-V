## Executive Summary

The new **About Us** feature for **BuildLab** was evaluated against the Anti-Slop Design & Copywriting specifications (`antislop.md`, `antislop-ui`, `antislop-copywriting`, `antislop-human`, and `antislop-layoutmobile`). 

The page presents the 5-developer student team (**WhiteHats**) from **Pateros Technological College (PTC)**. It includes interactive vitae dossiers, personal rig specifications, capstone contributions, and architecture breakdowns while adhering strictly to the Cyber-Industrial Hardware Lab theme established in `Home.jsx`.
